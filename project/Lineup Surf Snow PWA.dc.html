01

The tri-state information architecture

One skeleton, three states. Cam on top, verdict and why below, one glass deck in the thumb arc. Surf, CA Double and Snow are states of one screen, not three apps. The dock morphs; nothing reloads.

LW·EC

Context survives every switch. The playhead time, your break and your mountain carry across modes. Pick Swami’s in Surf and Bear Mountain in Snow, and CA Double already knows the trip.

DN

Same grammar in every mode: readout strip, scrubber, location pills and mode switch always sit in the same place. Only the lanes, the pills and the tokens change.

LW·JS

Depth by gesture. Scroll, or pull up the gear drawer, and the cam docks into a mini-bar. In CA Double the cam splits beach | mountain, or toggles to either feed.

EC
02

The California Double sync engine

One day, one track. Coastal window → transit bridge → alpine window. Each is a stage with its own window on the same 5 AM–5 PM axis the scrubber uses.

OT

The elevation lane is the map. In CA Double the scrubber’s line is you: at sea level while you surf, climbing as you drive, flat at the base lodge.

DN

Chains are checked for when you reach the grade, not for now. R1 on SR-38 at 7 AM can be R0 by an 11 AM arrival. Drive time includes rush hour, chain-up stops and an EV buffer.

OT·DN

Feasibility = 30% surf + 35% snow + 20% transit + 15% daylight fit. Flip the order and every stage, the score and the reason recompute.

03

PWA field resilience

Dead zones are the default. The shell, today’s forecasts and the last chain and lift status live in IndexedDB. Every cached value carries its timestamp, so “R1 · 6:31 AM” never pretends to be live.

LW

Glove mode. In Snow, targets grow from 48 to 56–64 pt, the scrubber snaps to 15-minute detents, and whole cells are tappable. No precision gestures, no long-press.

LW

Adaptive contrast tokens. OLED ocean slate for a 6 AM check, glare-resistant ice for 8,000 ft sun, a teal-to-frost horizon for CA Double. Quality colors are re-tuned per surface to hold 4.5:1.

JS

Springs, not easing curves. The drawer tracks your finger and releases with your velocity. prefers-reduced-motion snaps instead.

Test script

Monday, Jan 11. Sunday’s cold front left 9″ at Big Bear and a 15 s WNW groundswell at the coast.

  1. 01Switch modes in the dock: Surf · CA Double · Snow. Tokens, cam, lanes and pills morph. The playhead and your spots stay put.
  2. 02Flip Surf first ↔ Snow first. The score drops because the sea breeze blows out the afternoon surf.
  3. 03Tap Mammoth. Six hours on US-395 means “Not today”. Tap the break chip to change the origin.
  4. 04Scrub 5 AM → 5 PM. Energy, tide, drive time if you left then, summit wind and snow surface update together.
  5. 05Toggle the cam: split, beach or mountain. Scroll, or pull up the gear drawer, to dock it.
  6. 06Snow mode switches on glove-size targets automatically. Tweaks: clock (dawn or midday), offline, glove.

The cams are placeholders. Drop a still onto each feed; every spot keeps its own.
