01

Information architecture & flow

One screen, three lenses. The spot is global context, picked in the dock. Cams, Forecast and Map are lenses on that spot at the scrubbed moment. No hamburger, no nested menus, no settings in the path.

LW

Content top, controls bottom. Cam → verdict → why → tide sit in the reading zone. Scrubber, spots and tabs share one glass deck inside the thumb arc. Every target is at least 44 pt.

LW·JS

Zero-tap verdict. Launch lands on the last spot, live, already rated. Switch spot: 1 tap. Later today: 1 drag. Deep data: pull up. Best window: tap to jump.

OT

Depth by gesture, not pages. Scrolling docks the cam into a mini-bar. Pulling the deck up reveals swell trains and buoy telemetry and docks it too, so the cam is never lost and never in the way.

EC
02

The unified condition scrubber

One time axis, four lanes, read straight down the playhead: wind arrows drawn in the break’s own frame (↑ blowing out to sea = offshore), surf bars (height = face height, solid part = primary-swell share of the kJ), the tide curve, and hatched darkness before first and after last light.

OT·DN

Color means quality, nothing else. The tinted band behind the bars is the best window.

Good Fair Poor Flat / dark

Scrubbing is global time. The verdict, the why-rows, the tide marker, the swell trains, even the dots on every favorite in the dock re-rate for that 15-minute slot. Hours click like detents: a visual tick, plus vibration where the browser supports it.

LW·EC

Ratings are computed, not editorial: face height × wind relative to that beach’s orientation × the spot’s tide preference. That’s why “Why” can always show its work.

DN
03

PWA technical UX

Shell first, network last. A service worker precaches the app shell. Forecast JSON is stale-while-revalidate, and 7 days × every favorite is mirrored to IndexedDB. One bar on a cliff still gets a full, scrubbable forecast. Only the cam needs the network.

LW

Edge to edge, safely. Standalone display with viewport-fit=cover: the cam bleeds under the status bar behind a scrim, and the deck pads with env(safe-area-inset-bottom). theme-color #0A0E14 keeps OLED pixels dark.

EC

One progress value docks the cam, driven by scroll, sheet drag, tab change or the dock button. Compositor-friendly properties only, no layout thrash. Leaving the app hands the stream to system PiP (requestPictureInPicture).

EC

Springs, not easing curves. The deck tracks your finger and releases with your velocity. prefers-reduced-motion snaps instead.

Part 2 · live prototype

Test script

Persona: Monday, 6:20 AM, Blacks parking lot, 54°F, one cold thumb.

  1. 01Drag the timeline in the deck. Verdict, why, tide, swell trains and all four spot dots re-rate live. Pass 11 AM to watch Blacks blow out.
  2. 02Tap the best window card. The playhead jumps to 7:30 AM; “Now” brings it back.
  3. 03Scroll the verdict. The cam docks into a mini-bar and stays live. Tap the mini-bar to undock.
  4. 04Pull the deck up by its grabber, or tap Swell & buoy. You get primary vs secondary trains, the buoy, wetsuit and light. Flick it; it springs.
  5. 05Switch to Rincon to see the flat-spell state and its next rideable day. Scrub past 5:20 PM for after-dark.
  6. 06Open Tweaks. Clock → afternoon (blown out) or night (tomorrow’s dawn-patrol preview). Offline → cached forecast, cam on last frame.

The cam is a placeholder. Drop a real still onto it; each spot keeps its own.
